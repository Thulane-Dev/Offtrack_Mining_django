About Us

About Our Offtrack Mining

Offtrack Mining (Pty) Ltd is a reliable repairer and supplier of electrical, mechanical, and instrumentation equipment, serving the mining and industrial sectors. We specialise in the repair, refurbishment, mainte- nance, testing, and supply of critical equipment, including transformers, locomotive battery chargers, electric motors, pumps, idlers, pulleys, valves, actuators, positioners, and flow meters.

Our services are focused on restoring equipment to optimal operating condition, extending service life, and reducing downtime. With a commit- ment to quality, safety, and technical excellence, Offtrack Mining (Pty) Ltd provides cost-effective and dependable solutions that support efficient and reliable operations.
